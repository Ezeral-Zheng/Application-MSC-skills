# Program Fit and Evidence Matrix

Use this as a starting lens, then replace it with the target program's actual requirements when available.

| Program family | Foreground | Useful evidence | Usually compress |
|---|---|---|---|
| MiM / Management | ownership, coordination, leadership, operations, market decisions | team leadership, workflow redesign, customer/service coordination, strategy projects | isolated technical detail without a management consequence |
| Information Systems / Technology Management | technology adoption, process architecture, data quality, digital products, implementation | AI-assisted tools, system/process redesign, Spark or database work, data governance, product MVPs, cross-team delivery | finance detail that does not show a system or management implication |
| Financial Technology | finance + technology integration, quantitative analysis, risk/control, machine learning, data pipelines | econometrics, Python models, financial statement analysis, audit testing, Spark, financial product design | generic customer service and broad marketing detail |

## Scoring

Score each candidate from 0-3 for each dimension:

- direct target fit;
- factual strength and traceability;
- distinctiveness;
- quantified or observable result;
- space efficiency.

Prioritize high total scores. Keep one or two lower-scoring items only when they demonstrate a capability not otherwise represented, such as customer-facing coordination or team leadership.

## Selection Questions

For each retained item, answer:

1. What did the student personally do?
2. What system, method, tool, or decision was involved?
3. What changed, was measured, or was delivered?
4. Why does this matter for this target program?

If the answer depends on an unverified assumption, do not write the claim.
