To whom it may concern,

I am writing this letter to recommend one of my favourite students Zheng Junjie for your graduate program. Over the past academic year, Junjie has taken two courses I taught, namely Course Design for E-Business and Web/APP Design. Throughout this period, Junjie has demonstrated outstanding learning abilities, practical application skills, and a profound academic potential that left a lasting impression on me.

Junjie showed exceptional expertise and inventive skills in the implementation of practical applications throughout Course Design for E-Business. The training primarily focused on iterative improvements, utilizing user interviews to gather information about existing challenges, creating Persona analysis profiles, user behavior journey maps, and storyboards. He examined the organization of information from a functional perspective, performed an analysis of user flow, and ultimately developed prototypes. Junjie successfully executed the iterative upgrade project for an app as part of the course's main assignment. By leveraging user interaction experience and incorporating cutting-edge business models, he conducted simulations and analyses of the product's users, scenarios, and tasks, which not only showcases his comprehension of theoretical knowledge but also exemplifies his practical aptitude in implementing it in real-world projects.  
  
Likewise, in the Web/APP Design course, Junjie demonstrated outstanding performance. The primary goal of this course is to gain a profound understanding of the design principles and optimization methodologies employed in Internet products. By engaging in systematic learning, Junjie acquired a comprehensive understanding of the fundamental principles of product design, the methodologies and approaches of user experience design, and the strategies for analyzing and optimizing product data. He demonstrated exceptional performance in both completing major tasks with excellent quality and actively participating in classroom discussions. He consistently responded to queries, actively participated in conversations, and frequently took the initiative to present his projects.

During our interactions and from my observations, Junjie displayed a number of crucial skills that are especially significant for his application such as innovative thinking and problem-solving abilities, proficiency in data analysis and optimization, technical application abilities in practical projects and strong teamwork and leadership skills. I am confident that he will continue to demonstrate exceptional performance in your graduate program and attain even more remarkable achievements. I strongly recommend Junjie and trust that your university will afford him the opportunity to pursue advanced academic pursuits.

Yours sincerely,

Li Junjun, Associate Professor

Hangzhou Dianzi University
