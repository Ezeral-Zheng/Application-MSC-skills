# Resume Review Checklist

## Full Resume

- Education information matches Basic_Info.
- Experience entries match Resume, Course_Info, or user-provided facts.
- No fabricated scores, rankings, tools, datasets, companies, titles, awards, dates, or outcomes.
- Bullets are concise, action-driven, and not written like PS paragraphs.
- Section order supports the target program or use case.

## Resume-Experience

- Every project comes from Course_Info.
- Incomplete projects are excluded from正文 and listed in `未纳入项目`.
- Role and date are omitted unless supported.
- `资料依据` lists all Course_Info files used.
- Project formatting follows `resume-experience-format.md`.
